About Us

Who we are

Sixfold Capital brings together a team of six with a shared commitment to rigorous research and disciplined portfolio construction.

We combine macroeconomic insight with quantitative analysis to develop a global multi-asset approach. Economic reasoning guides the exposures we consider, while portfolio analysis helps us assess diversification, correlations and contributions to risk.

Our philosophy

Macroeconomic research at the foundation of every strategic allocation.

Our approach

Three disciplines, one process.

01 — MACRO

Macroeconomic research

We assess growth, inflation, monetary policy and geopolitical risks to develop our outlook and alternative scenarios.

02 — QUANTITATIVE

Quantitative analysis

We compare allocation approaches and examine how weights, correlations and risk contributions shape the portfolio.

03 — RISK

Diversification & risk

We evaluate exposures across asset classes and regions, and the conditions in which diversification may weaken.
