Investment Approach

From economic outlook to portfolio decisions

Our investment process connects a view of the global economy with explicit choices about portfolio exposures, allocation and risk.

01

Define the investment case

For each proposed exposure, we set out the investment rationale, the assumptions it depends on and the developments that would lead us to reconsider it.

02

Select the instruments

We compare eligible ETFs by underlying exposure, liquidity, costs and currency characteristics. Each selected instrument must serve a clear purpose within the allocation.

03

Determine portfolio weights

We compare alternative weighting methods and assess their implications for the portfolio’s risk profile. We then test the proposed allocation against the volatility mandate and examine how it would have behaved historically.

Investment Parameters

Our Round 1 portfolio operates within a simulated USD 100 million mandate, using eligible long-only ETFs and targeting 8% annual volatility, within an admissible range of 7–9%.
