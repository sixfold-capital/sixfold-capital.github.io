ROUND 1

Strategic Asset Allocation

Our Round 1 allocation holds six US-listed ETFs in two blocks: a growth block of US equities, US energy and currency-hedged emerging markets, and a protection block of short-term inflation-linked Treasuries, intermediate Treasuries and gold.

Weights come from two-level risk budgeting at an ex ante volatility of 8%, the centre of the mandate’s 7–9% range. The growth block carries about 80% of the portfolio risk; the protection block carries the remaining 20%, split equally across its three ETFs.

We compared the allocation with two simple alternatives built from the same ETFs. An equal-weight portfolio reaches an ex ante volatility of 9.0%, at the top of the mandate’s range, while a risk-parity portfolio falls to 5.3%, well below it.

The report, code and portfolio description were delivered on 18 October 2026. The portfolio at inception and the backtest results are on the Performance page.
